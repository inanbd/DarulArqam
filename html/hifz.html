Overview

Darul Arqam offers a structured Hifz Program designed to develop students’ ability to memorize, recite, revise and retain the Holy Qur’an while gaining essential Islamic knowledge.

Our program goes beyond Qur’an memorization. Alongside their Hifz studies, all students receive systematic instruction in Aqidah, Fiqh, Hadith, Seerah, Islamic manners, daily supplications and other essential aspects of Islamic education.

Our aim is to nurture students who not only memorize the words of the Qur’an but also understand the fundamentals of their faith and apply Islamic teachings in their everyday lives.

  • Track A · The entire Qur’an: 30 Juz

    Complete Hifz

    The Complete Hifz Program aims to enable students to memorize the entire Holy Qur’an (30 Juz) with accuracy, proper pronunciation and long-term retention.

    • Memorization of the entire Holy Qur’an (30 Juz)
    • Structured daily memorization
    • Comprehensive revision system
    • Tajweed and pronunciation development
    • Regular examinations and progress monitoring

  • Track B · Chosen Surahs and passages

    Selected Surahs Hifz

    This program is designed for students who wish to memorize selected Surahs and important portions of the Holy Qur’an without undertaking the complete 30-Juz memorization track.

    • Memorization of selected Qur’anic Surahs and passages
    • Individualized memorization targets
    • Pronunciation and Tajweed development
    • Regular revision and retention exercises
    • Progress-based assessment

How lessons run

Three parts to every lesson

New memorization is only kept by revising it. Every Hifz lesson therefore has three parts.

  1. 01

    Sabaq

    New memorization

    • Memorization of new Qur’anic verses
    • Correct pronunciation of words and letters
    • Proper connection between verses
    • Memorization with attention to similar verses (Mutashabihat)
    • Accurate recitation without relying on the Mushaf
    • Gradual development of memorization capacity
  2. 02

    Sabqi

    Recent revision

    • Recite previously memorized lessons accurately
    • Connect newly memorized portions with earlier lessons
    • Identify and correct memorization mistakes
    • Maintain continuity between consecutive passages
    • Reduce dependence on teacher prompts
  3. 03

    Manzil

    Long-term revision

    • Systematic revision of older memorized portions
    • Maintaining fluency throughout the Qur’an
    • Identifying forgotten verses
    • Correcting recurring mistakes
    • Strengthening long-term retention
    • Developing independent revision habits

Teachers will assign new lessons according to individual ability and progress.

Tajweed and Makharij

  1. Makharij al-Huruf (articulation points)
  2. Sifat al-Huruf (characteristics of letters)
  3. Basic rules of Noon Sakinah and Tanween
  4. Rules of Meem Sakinah
  5. Rules of Madd (elongation)
  6. Qalqalah
  7. Rules of Lam and Ra
  8. Characteristics of heavy and light letters
  9. Rules of Waqf and Ibtida (stopping and starting)
  10. Common recitation errors and their correction

The level of instruction will be adjusted according to the student’s existing knowledge and recitation ability.

Trained for accuracy

  • Memorize verses in their correct sequence
  • Distinguish between similar verses
  • Maintain accurate word order
  • Recite without unnecessary hesitation
  • Correct omissions, additions and substitutions
  • Maintain consistency in pronunciation

Track A · Complete Hifz

Four stages to the whole Qur’an

Progression is based on demonstrated ability rather than a fixed time limit.

  1. 1

    Foundation and Readiness

    • Assessment of Qur’an reading ability
    • Evaluation of pronunciation and Tajweed
    • Correction of essential recitation errors
    • Establishment of daily memorization habits
    • Development of an individualized learning plan
  2. 2

    Progressive Memorization

    • Systematic memorization of assigned Qur’anic portions
    • Daily Sabaq
    • Regular Sabqi
    • Scheduled Manzil
    • Continuous teacher feedback
  3. 3

    Consolidation and Revision

    • Strengthening memorized portions
    • Increasing revision consistency
    • Improving fluency
    • Identifying weak portions
    • Preparing for cumulative assessments
  4. 4

    Completion and Final Assessment

    • Completion of the entire 30 Juz
    • Comprehensive revision
    • Oral examinations
    • Evaluation of memorization accuracy
    • Assessment of Tajweed and recitation
    • Final Hifz completion evaluation

Certification

A student may qualify for Hifz completion certification after:

  • Completing memorization of all 30 Juz
  • Completing the required revision
  • Passing the final Hifz examination
  • Demonstrating satisfactory recitation accuracy

Students may receive a program completion certificate after completing their agreed memorization objectives and passing the relevant assessment.
