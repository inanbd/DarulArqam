About

Our name

Named after the first school of Islam

In the earliest days of Islam, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ gathered his companions in a modest house at the foot of Mount Safa in Makkah — the home of al-Arqam ibn Abi al-Arqam, may Allah be pleased with him. There, away from the noise of the city, the Qur’an was taught from heart to heart. That house came to be known as Dar al-Arqam: the first school of Islam.

Nazirah

One-on-one · Children, teenagers and adults

Read the Qur’an correctly, fluently and with confidence

Darul Arqam’s Nazirah Program is designed to help students develop the ability to read the Holy Qur’an accurately, fluently and confidently while applying the essential rules of Tajweed.

Students work towards

  • Recognizing Arabic letters and their correct pronunciation
  • Developing fluency in Qur’anic reading
  • Learning essential Tajweed rules
  • Improving recitation accuracy
  • Building confidence through regular practice

18

Nazirah

The stages

  1. 1

    Arabic Alphabet and Basic Foundations

    Students should be able to recognize Arabic letters, identify their different forms and pronounce them correctly with appropriate articulation.

  2. 2

    Basic Qur’anic Reading and Qaida

    Students should be able to read connected Arabic words and demonstrate sufficient reading ability to begin the Holy Qur’an.

  3. 3

    Introduction to Tajweed

    Students should understand and apply the essential Tajweed rules appropriate to their reading level.

  2. 5

    Fluency, Accuracy and Independent Recitation

    Students should be able to read the Holy Qur’an independently with satisfactory fluency, pronunciation and Tajweed.

    Individualized memorization targets · Pronunciation and Tajweed development · Regular revision and retention exercises

24

Hifz

How lessons run

Three parts to every lesson

  1. 1

    SabaqNew memorization

    Memorization of new Qur’anic verses · Correct pronunciation of words and letters · Proper connection between verses

  2. 2

    SabqiRecent revision

    Recite previously memorized lessons accurately · Connect newly memorized portions with earlier lessons · Identify and correct memorization mistakes

  3. 3

    ManzilLong-term revision

    Systematic revision of older memorized portions · Maintaining fluency throughout the Qur’an · Identifying forgotten verses

New memorization is only kept by revising it. Every Hifz lesson therefore has three parts.

25

Hifz

Track A · Complete Hifz

Four stages to the whole Qur’an

  1. 1

    Foundation and Readiness

    Assessment of Qur’an reading ability · Evaluation of pronunciation and Tajweed · Correction of essential recitation errors

  2. 2

    Progressive Memorization

    Systematic memorization of assigned Qur’anic portions · Daily Sabaq · Regular Sabqi

  3. 3

    Consolidation and Revision

    Strengthening memorized portions · Increasing revision consistency · Improving fluency

  4. 4

    Completion and Final Assessment

    Completion of the entire 30 Juz · Comprehensive revision · Oral examinations

FAQ

Answers for parents and students

Which programs do you offer?

Three. Nazirah teaches a student to read the Qur’an correctly and fluently. Hifz is memorization, either of the whole Qur’an or of selected Surahs. Alimiyah and Islamic Studies is a structured course of Islamic knowledge in three divisions, from Junior Islamic Studies for children to an Adult Alimiyah.

How do the online classes work?

Nazirah and Hifz are live, private video lessons between one student and one teacher, who listens to the student recite and corrects gently as they go. Alimiyah and Junior Islamic Studies are taught in group classes, semester by semester. You can join from a laptop, tablet or phone.

What is Deeniyat?

Deeniyat is the Islamic studies that every Nazirah and Hifz student takes alongside the Qur’an: Aqidah, practical Fiqh, Hadith and Sunnah, Seerah, manners and character, daily duas, Islamic history and a basic understanding of the Qur’an. It is a compulsory part of both programs.

FAQ

Questions

What ages do you teach?

Nazirah and Hifz are open to children, teenagers and adults. Junior Islamic Studies is for ages 3 to 12, the Youth and Young Adults Alimiyah for ages 12 to 25, and the Adult Alimiyah for ages 25 and above. Placement follows a student’s readiness and previous learning, not age alone.

Can my daughter or I learn with a female teacher?

Yes. Qualified female teachers are available for sisters and girls. Simply choose “Female teacher” when you request your trial class.

What language are classes taught in?

English is the main language of instruction. Arabic is taught as a subject and used more and more for reading Islamic texts. Where it is needed, a teacher may clarify a point in another language.

61

FAQ

Questions

How will I know how my child is doing?

Parents receive periodic progress reports covering reading or memorization, revision, recitation quality, Deeniyat, attendance, the teacher’s feedback and the areas that need more work.

Do students receive a certificate?

Yes, on completing a program or stage and passing its assessment. A certificate states the program and level actually completed. It is a certificate of completion from Darul Arqam, not an accredited degree or a government-recognized qualification.

Will the class times suit my time zone?

Yes. Classes are scheduled in your local time, anywhere in the United States, and students in other countries are welcome too. Because evening in America is morning in Bangladesh, after-school and evening slots work especially well.
