About

Our name

Named after the first school of Islam

In the earliest days of Islam, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ gathered his companions in a modest house at the foot of Mount Safa in Makkah — the home of al-Arqam ibn Abi al-Arqam, may Allah be pleased with him. There, away from the noise of the city, the Quran was taught from heart to heart. That house came to be known as Dar al-Arqam: the first school of Islam.

Programs

01 اِقْرَأْ

Quran Reading

From Noorani Qaida to fluent reading

Begin with the Arabic letters and their sounds through the Noorani Qaida, then move step by step to reading the Quran fluently (Nazra) and with confidence.

What you will learn

  • Arabic letters and their correct articulation
  • Joining letters, vowels and the first rules
  • Guided reading from the Mushaf

For Beginners of any age

02 تَرْتِيل

Tajweed

Recite correctly and beautifully

Learn the rules of recitation and apply them verse by verse, with your teacher listening and correcting you in real time.

What you will learn

  • Makharij and sifaat of every letter
  • Rules of noon, meem, madd and waqf
  • Applied practice with live correction

For Students who can already read

03 حِفْظ

Hifz

Memorize with a hafiz by your side

A structured memorization program built on the daily rhythm of new lesson, recent revision and older revision — the method of Bangladesh’s hifz madrasahs.

What you will learn

  • Daily sabaq, sabqi and manzil
  • A personal goal: selected surahs, a juz or the whole Quran
  • Regular progress updates for parents

For Children and adults ready to commit

  • Basic Quranic Arabic and vocabulary

Teachers

Our teachers

Imams and huffaz, rooted in a living tradition

Bangladesh is home to one of the world’s richest traditions of Quran memorization, and its huffaz regularly earn top honors at international Quran competitions.

Our teachers come from that tradition: imams and huffaz who have devoted years to learning the Quran and to teaching it in mosques and madrasahs — and who now bring the same care to your family’s lessons.

FAQ

Questions

Answers for parents and students

How do the online classes work?

Each class is a live, private video call between the student and the teacher. The teacher shares the lesson on screen, listens to the student recite, and corrects gently as they go. You can join from a laptop, tablet or phone.

What ages do you teach?

We teach children from around five years old, as well as teenagers and adults of any age. Lessons for young children are kept short and lively.

28

FAQ

Questions

Can my daughter or I learn with a female teacher?

Yes. Qualified female teachers are available for sisters and girls. Simply choose “Female teacher” when you request your trial class.

What language are lessons taught in?

Lessons can be taught in English or Bangla. Tell us which the student is most comfortable with and we will match a teacher accordingly.

Will the class times suit my time zone?

Yes. Classes are scheduled in your local time, anywhere in the United States. Because evening in America is morning in Bangladesh, after-school and evening slots work especially well.

FAQ

Questions

What do we need for a class?

A device with a camera and microphone, a steady internet connection and a quiet place to sit. For reading lessons, a printed Qaida or Mushaf beside the student is helpful.

What if we have to miss a class?

Let your teacher know ahead of time and we will do our best to arrange a make-up class.
