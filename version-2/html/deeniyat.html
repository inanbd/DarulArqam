Comprehensive Islamic studies

Deeniyat is a compulsory part of both the Nazirah and the Hifz programs.

Its purpose is to develop students’ understanding of Islam, strengthen their beliefs, teach essential religious obligations and encourage the practical application of Islamic teachings.

We believe that a successful Qur’an student should not only memorize the words of Allah but also understand the foundations of faith, learn how to worship correctly and develop good Islamic character.

  1. 01 عَقِيدَة

    Aqidah

    Islamic creed

    To develop a sound understanding of fundamental Islamic beliefs and the essential principles of faith.

    • Introduction to Islam
    • Belief in Allah
    • Belief in Angels
    • Belief in Divine Books
    • Belief in Prophets
    • Belief in the Hereafter
    • Belief in Qadr

    By the end: Students should understand the fundamental articles of faith, recognize the importance of Tawheed, distinguish between basic concepts of belief and disbelief, and develop a stronger relationship with Allah.

    What is covered

    Introduction to Islam

    • Meaning and significance of Islam
    • The meaning of Iman
    • The five pillars of Islam
    • The six articles of faith
    • The relationship between belief and action

    Belief in Allah

    • Allah’s existence and Oneness
    • Tawheed (the Oneness of Allah)
    • Allah’s names and attributes
    • Allah as the Creator and Sustainer
    • Worship and sincere devotion to Allah
    • Shirk and its basic forms
    • The importance of relying upon Allah

    Belief in Angels

    • The creation and nature of angels
    • Angels as obedient servants of Allah
    • Names and responsibilities of well-known angels
    • The recording of human deeds
    • The role of angels in Islamic belief

    Belief in Divine Books

    • The concept of revelation
    • The revealed scriptures
    • The Tawrat, Zabur, Injil and the Qur’an
    • The Qur’an as the final revealed Book
    • The importance of following the Qur’an

    Belief in Prophets

    • The meaning of Prophethood
    • The purpose of sending Prophets
    • Important Prophets mentioned in the Qur’an
    • The finality of Prophethood
    • The special status of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
    • Respect and love for all Prophets

    Belief in the Hereafter

    • Death and the life of Barzakh
    • Resurrection
    • Gathering on the Day of Judgment
    • Accountability for deeds
    • Paradise and Hell
    • The importance of preparing for the Hereafter

    Belief in Qadr

    • Meaning of Divine Decree
    • Allah’s knowledge and wisdom
    • Human responsibility and choices
    • Patience and gratitude
    • Trust in Allah
