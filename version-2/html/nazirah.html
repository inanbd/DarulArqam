Read the Qur’an correctly, fluently and with confidence

From the first Arabic letter to independent recitation with Tajweed, in five stages and at the student’s own pace.

  • Online · One-on-one
  • Children, teenagers and adults
  • 5 stages

Overview

Darul Arqam’s Nazirah Program is designed to help students develop the ability to read the Holy Qur’an accurately, fluently and confidently while applying the essential rules of Tajweed.

The program begins with the fundamentals of Arabic letters and pronunciation and gradually progresses towards fluent Qur’anic recitation.

Alongside Qur’an reading, every student receives structured Islamic education (Deeniyat), covering essential Islamic beliefs, practical religious rulings, Hadith, Seerah, Islamic manners and daily supplications.

Our objective is to develop students who can read the Qur’an correctly and understand the essential teachings of Islam.

Students work towards

  • Recognizing Arabic letters and their correct pronunciation
  • Developing fluency in Qur’anic reading
  • Learning essential Tajweed rules
  • Improving recitation accuracy
  • Building confidence through regular practice

The stages

From the first letter to independent recitation

Students move on when they are ready, not by the calendar.

  1. 1
    ا Stage 1

    Arabic Alphabet and Basic Foundations

    To introduce students to the Arabic alphabet and establish a strong foundation for Qur’anic reading.

    By the end: Students should be able to recognize Arabic letters, identify their different forms and pronounce them correctly with appropriate articulation.

    What is covered

    Introduction to Arabic Letters

    • Recognition of all 29 Arabic letters
    • Names and basic characteristics of letters
    • Understanding the different forms of Arabic letters
    • Recognition of letters at the beginning, middle and end of words
    • Identifying similarities between Arabic letters

    Letter Pronunciation

    • Introduction to Makharij al-Huruf
    • Correct pronunciation of individual letters
    • Distinguishing between similar-sounding letters
    • Correct articulation of difficult Arabic sounds
    • Practical pronunciation exercises

    Basic Reading Signs

    • Fathah, Kasrah and Dammah
    • Tanween
    • Sukoon
    • Shaddah
    • Basic combinations of Arabic letters
    • Reading simple Arabic words
  2. 2
    بَ Stage 2

    Basic Qur’anic Reading and Qaida

    To develop the ability to combine Arabic letters and read connected words and simple Qur’anic passages.

    By the end: Students should be able to read connected Arabic words and demonstrate sufficient reading ability to begin the Holy Qur’an.

    Students may use an appropriate Qaida textbook as their initial learning resource.

    What is covered
    • Combining Arabic letters
    • Reading words with Fathah, Kasrah and Dammah
    • Reading words containing Tanween
    • Reading words containing Sukoon
    • Reading words with Shaddah
    • Recognition of Madd letters
    • Basic rules of elongation
    • Reading connected Arabic words
    • Understanding basic reading patterns
    • Introduction to Qur’anic reading signs
  3. 3
    مَدّ Stage 3

    Introduction to Tajweed

    To introduce the essential rules of Tajweed and help students apply them during Qur’anic recitation.

    By the end: Students should understand and apply the essential Tajweed rules appropriate to their reading level.

    What is covered

    Makharij al-Huruf

    • Introduction to articulation points
    • Correct pronunciation of Arabic letters
    • Major articulation areas
    • Distinguishing similar letters
    • Practical pronunciation correction

    Sifat al-Huruf

    • Introduction to letter characteristics
    • Heavy and light letters
    • Qalqalah letters
    • Basic letter characteristics
    • Correct application during recitation

    Noon Sakinah and Tanween

    • Izhar
    • Idgham
    • Iqlab
    • Ikhfa
    • Practical identification and application

    Meem Sakinah

    • Ikhfa Shafawi
    • Idgham Shafawi
    • Izhar Shafawi
    • Practical recitation exercises

    Rules of Madd

    • Madd Asli (Natural Madd)
    • Madd Far’i (Secondary Madd)
    • Common types of Madd
    • Appropriate elongation
    • Practical application

    Other Essential Tajweed Rules

    • Qalqalah
    • Rules of Lam
    • Rules of Ra
    • Rules of Hamzat al-Wasl
    • Basic rules of Waqf and Ibtida
    • Common Tajweed mistakes
  4. 4
    رَتِّلِ Stage 4

    Qur’an Reading and Recitation Development

    To develop accurate, fluent and confident Qur’anic reading.

    By the end: Students should be able to read the Holy Qur’an accurately, fluently and with appropriate application of Tajweed.

    Students will progress according to their individual reading ability and performance.

    What is covered
    • Guided reading of the Holy Qur’an
    • Gradual progression through Qur’anic passages
    • Correct pronunciation of words and verses
    • Application of Tajweed rules
    • Proper articulation of letters
    • Recognition of common recitation mistakes
    • Correcting reading errors
    • Developing reading fluency
    • Maintaining appropriate reading pace
    • Practicing proper stopping and restarting
    • Reading independently with improved confidence
  5. 5
    اقْرَأْ Stage 5

    Fluency, Accuracy and Independent Recitation

    To strengthen students’ reading ability and establish consistency in correct Qur’anic recitation.

    By the end: Students should be able to read the Holy Qur’an independently with satisfactory fluency, pronunciation and Tajweed.

    What is covered
    • Continuous Qur’an reading practice
    • Improved reading speed without compromising accuracy
    • Consistent Tajweed application
    • Correction of recurring pronunciation errors
    • Proper Waqf and Ibtida
    • Reading unfamiliar passages with confidence
    • Independent recitation
    • Regular reading assessments
    • Development of personal Qur’an reading habits
