How we teach, assess and care for students

Knowledge, understanding, practice and character: the principles behind every lesson, and the standards we hold ourselves to.

Educational philosophy

Four principles behind every lesson

At Darul Arqam, education is not limited to memorizing information. We believe that meaningful Islamic education should develop knowledge, understanding, practice and character.

  1. 1

    Knowledge

    Acquiring essential Islamic knowledge through structured learning.

  2. 2

    Understanding

    Developing the ability to understand and reflect upon what is learned.

  3. 3

    Practice

    Encouraging students to apply Islamic teachings in their daily lives.

  4. 4

    Character

    Developing good manners, responsibility and a strong sense of accountability.

We aim to support students according to their individual learning levels, abilities and educational needs.

Teaching and learning

We believe that effective education requires clear objectives, organized lessons, appropriate teaching methods and regular feedback.

  • Structured lesson planning
  • Student-centered instruction
  • Teaching appropriate to the student’s level
  • Regular revision and reinforcement
  • Constructive feedback
  • Consistent monitoring of learning outcomes

Teachers are expected to maintain a respectful, professional and supportive relationship with their students.

Teaching methods

  • Direct instruction
  • Guided reading of Islamic texts
  • Explanation and discussion
  • Question-and-answer
  • Memorization and revision
  • Practical demonstrations
  • Written assignments
  • Group activities where appropriate
  • Case-based learning
  • Research and presentations at advanced levels

Assessment and student progress

Student progress is an essential part of our academic approach. Assessment methods are selected according to the nature of each program and may include:

  • Regular lesson assessments
  • Oral recitation and memorization tests
  • Written examinations
  • Revision assessments
  • Periodic progress reviews

The purpose of assessment is not only to measure performance but also to identify learning needs and help students improve.

Where appropriate, parents or guardians may receive updates regarding their child’s attendance, performance and academic progress.

Faculty and teaching standards

We recognize that teachers play a central role in the quality of Islamic education.

Darul Arqam seeks to engage teachers who demonstrate appropriate subject knowledge, teaching ability, professional conduct and commitment to student development.

  • Adequate knowledge of the subjects being taught
  • Clear and effective communication
  • Responsible lesson preparation
  • Fair and respectful treatment of students
  • Regular monitoring of student progress
  • Commitment to continuous professional development

Advanced Tafsir, Hadith methodology, Usul al-Fiqh and advanced Fiqh should be taught by instructors with appropriate subject expertise.

Student welfare and learning environment

We believe that students learn best in an environment that is respectful, organized and supportive.

  • Respectful interaction between teachers and students
  • A safe and supportive learning environment
  • Appropriate student conduct and discipline
  • Respect for privacy and dignity
  • Fair treatment and equal educational consideration
  • Gender-appropriate class arrangements

Our aim is to support students’ academic, personal and moral development.

Progression

Progression is based on demonstrated learning outcomes. A student may be promoted, retained for further study, assigned foundational lessons or supplementary tasks, or reassessed for a different level. Academic readiness, not age or attendance alone, determines progression.

Academic integrity

Students must submit their own work, avoid plagiarism, use sources responsibly, respect examination rules, avoid misrepresenting performance and maintain honesty in research and assignments.

Certification

Certificates reflect the program and academic level actually completed.

They are certificates of completion issued by Darul Arqam. They are not an accredited degree or a government-recognized qualification, and are not presented as equivalent to another institution’s Alimiyah or Dars-e-Nizami qualification.

An international learning community

Darul Arqam aims to make Islamic education accessible to students across different countries and communities.

We recognize that students may come from different educational, cultural and linguistic backgrounds. Therefore, we seek to provide an organized learning experience that takes individual circumstances into consideration.

Our educational approach values mutual respect, clear communication and the shared pursuit of Islamic knowledge.

Always improving

Education is an ongoing process. We are committed to reviewing our academic practices, improving teaching methods and developing our educational programs over time.

Our long-term objective is to build a well-organized Islamic educational institution that maintains meaningful academic standards while remaining responsive to the needs of students and families.
