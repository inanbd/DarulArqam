Who we teach

A place for every learner in your family

  • Children

    From the first Arabic letters to fluent reading and memorization, with Junior Islamic Studies for ages 3 to 12 and regular progress reports for parents.

  • Teenagers and young adults

    Qur’an programs at their own level, and the Youth Alimiyah: a six-semester path through Qur’an, Hadith, Aqidah, Fiqh and Arabic for ages 12 to 25.

  • Adults

    It is never too late. Private Qur’an lessons at your own pace, and an Adult Alimiyah paced around work and family.

Qualified female teachers are available for sisters and girls.
