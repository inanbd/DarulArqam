A clear path, from the first letter to lifelong learning

Three programs: read the Qur’an, memorize it, and study the knowledge of Islam. Every Qur’an student also takes Deeniyat.

  1. 01

    Nazirah al-Qur’an

    Learn to read the Qur’an correctly

    The Nazirah program focuses on developing students’ ability to read the Holy Qur’an accurately and confidently. The program is structured according to each student’s current reading ability and progress.

    • One-on-one lessons
    • Children, teenagers and adults
    View the program
    اِقْرَأْ

    Students work towards

    • Recognizing Arabic letters and their correct pronunciation
    • Developing fluency in Qur’anic reading
    • Learning essential Tajweed rules
    • Improving recitation accuracy
    • Building confidence through regular practice
  2. 02

    Hifz al-Qur’an

    Memorize the Qur’an and keep it

    The Hifz program is designed for students who wish to memorize the Holy Qur’an. Our aim is to help students establish a strong and lasting relationship with the Qur’an.

    • One-on-one lessons
    • Children, teenagers and adults
    View the program
    حِفْظ

    The program emphasizes

    • Accurate memorization
    • Proper pronunciation and recitation
    • Regular revision and retention
    • Consistent lesson planning
    • Individual progress monitoring
    • Development of discipline and commitment
  3. 03

    Alimiyah and Islamic Studies

    Structured Islamic knowledge for every stage of life

    The Alimiyah program is designed for students seeking a structured and broader understanding of Islamic knowledge. The program aims to develop a sound foundation in Islamic scholarship, understanding and academic study.

    • Group classes by semester
    • Ages 3 to adult, in three divisions
    View the program
    عِلْم

    Depending on the approved curriculum and level, subjects may include

    • Qur’an and Tafsir
    • Hadith and its foundational sciences
    • Aqidah (Islamic Creed)
    • Fiqh (Islamic Jurisprudence)
    • Seerah (Life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ)
    • Arabic Language and related disciplines
دِين

Deeniyat, with every Qur’an program

Nazirah and Hifz students also study eight subjects of essential Islamic knowledge, from Aqidah and Fiqh to manners and daily duas.

  • Aqidah
  • Fiqh
  • Hadith and Sunnah
  • Seerah
  • Akhlaq
  • Duas and Adhkar
  • Islamic History
  • Qur’anic Understanding

Find the right program

Where to begin, by age

Placement follows a student’s readiness and previous learning, not age alone. As a guide:

  • Ages 3–12

    • Nazirah al-Qur’an
    • Hifz al-Qur’an
    • Junior Islamic Studies

  • Ages 12–25

    • Nazirah al-Qur’an
    • Hifz al-Qur’an
    • Youth and Young Adults Alimiyah

  • Ages 25+

    • Nazirah al-Qur’an
    • Hifz al-Qur’an
    • Adult Alimiyah

Who we teach

A place for every learner in your family

  • Children

    From the first Arabic letters to fluent reading and memorization, with Junior Islamic Studies for ages 3 to 12 and regular progress reports for parents.

  • Teenagers and young adults

    Qur’an programs at their own level, and the Youth Alimiyah: a six-semester path through Qur’an, Hadith, Aqidah, Fiqh and Arabic for ages 12 to 25.

  • Adults

    It is never too late. Private Qur’an lessons at your own pace, and an Adult Alimiyah paced around work and family.

Qualified female teachers are available for sisters and girls.
