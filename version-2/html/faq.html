Answers for parents and students

Something else on your mind? Message us and we will gladly help.

Which programs do you offer?

Three. Nazirah teaches a student to read the Qur’an correctly and fluently. Hifz is memorization, either of the whole Qur’an or of selected Surahs. Alimiyah and Islamic Studies is a structured course of Islamic knowledge in three divisions, from Junior Islamic Studies for children to an Adult Alimiyah.

How do the online classes work?

Nazirah and Hifz are live, private video lessons between one student and one teacher, who listens to the student recite and corrects gently as they go. Alimiyah and Junior Islamic Studies are taught in group classes, semester by semester. You can join from a laptop, tablet or phone.

What is Deeniyat?

Deeniyat is the Islamic studies that every Nazirah and Hifz student takes alongside the Qur’an: Aqidah, practical Fiqh, Hadith and Sunnah, Seerah, manners and character, daily duas, Islamic history and a basic understanding of the Qur’an. It is a compulsory part of both programs.

What ages do you teach?

Nazirah and Hifz are open to children, teenagers and adults. Junior Islamic Studies is for ages 3 to 12, the Youth and Young Adults Alimiyah for ages 12 to 25, and the Adult Alimiyah for ages 25 and above. Placement follows a student’s readiness and previous learning, not age alone.

Can my daughter or I learn with a female teacher?

Yes. Qualified female teachers are available for sisters and girls. Simply choose “Female teacher” when you request your trial class.

What language are classes taught in?

English is the main language of instruction. Arabic is taught as a subject and used more and more for reading Islamic texts. Where it is needed, a teacher may clarify a point in another language.

How will I know how my child is doing?

Parents receive periodic progress reports covering reading or memorization, revision, recitation quality, Deeniyat, attendance, the teacher’s feedback and the areas that need more work.

Do students receive a certificate?

Yes, on completing a program or stage and passing its assessment. A certificate states the program and level actually completed. It is a certificate of completion from Darul Arqam, not an accredited degree or a government-recognized qualification.

Will the class times suit my time zone?

Yes. Classes are scheduled in your local time, anywhere in the United States, and students in other countries are welcome too. Because evening in America is morning in Bangladesh, after-school and evening slots work especially well.

What do we need for a class?

A device with a camera and microphone, a steady internet connection and a quiet place to sit. For reading lessons, a printed Qaida or Mushaf beside the student is helpful.

What if we have to miss a class?

Let your teacher know ahead of time and we will do our best to arrange a make-up class.
