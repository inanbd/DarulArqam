A house of Qur’an, carried across the ocean

We are named after the house where the Prophet ﷺ taught his first companions, and built to bring that same teacher-to-student learning into your home.

Our name

Named after the first school of Islam

In the earliest days of Islam, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ gathered his companions in a modest house at the foot of Mount Safa in Makkah — the home of al-Arqam ibn Abi al-Arqam, may Allah be pleased with him. There, away from the noise of the city, the Qur’an was taught from heart to heart. That house came to be known as Dar al-Arqam: the first school of Islam.

Darul Arqam carries that spirit across the ocean. We connect families in the United States with imams and huffaz in Bangladesh — a land where learning and teaching the Qur’an is a living tradition, passed from teacher to student, generation after generation.

Our vision

To become a trusted Islamic educational institution that nurtures knowledgeable, principled and responsible individuals through quality Islamic education and Qur’anic learning.

Our academic approach combines the preservation of authentic Islamic knowledge with effective teaching methods, continuous learning and character development.

We aim to create a learning environment in which students can develop their relationship with the Qur’an, strengthen their understanding of Islam and grow into responsible individuals guided by Islamic values.

Our mission

An international learning community

Darul Arqam aims to make Islamic education accessible to students across different countries and communities.

We recognize that students may come from different educational, cultural and linguistic backgrounds. Therefore, we seek to provide an organized learning experience that takes individual circumstances into consideration.

Our educational approach values mutual respect, clear communication and the shared pursuit of Islamic knowledge.

Always improving

Education is an ongoing process. We are committed to reviewing our academic practices, improving teaching methods and developing our educational programs over time.

Our long-term objective is to build a well-organized Islamic educational institution that maintains meaningful academic standards while remaining responsive to the needs of students and families.
